The group

Founded in Limassol in 1979, Interorient has grown into a diversified maritime group with ships, capital and people at sea worldwide.

Two generations of the Papadopoulos family have led the company, building on a culture of trust, reliability and long-term partnership.

Heritage

1979

Almost half a century of owning, managing and partnering at sea.

  1. 1979

    Founded in Limassol

    Adonis Papadopoulos establishes Interorient as a shipowner in Limassol. Interorient Shipmanagement begins the same year, managing the group's ships and, in time, vessels for third-party owners.

  2. 2004

    The second generation

    Themis Papadopoulos succeeds his father as Chief Executive, leading the group's growth into ship management, pools and funds.

  3. 2005

    Norient Product Pool

    Interorient and Denmark's NORDEN establish the Norient Product Pool, today the NORDEN Tanker Pool, one of the world's largest MR and Handysize product tanker pools.

  4. 2007 – 2008

    A fleet of 100 ships

    Interorient's owned fleet reaches 100 vessels: product tankers and bulk carriers alongside containerships, multipurpose and ro-ro vessels.

  5. 2019

    40 years of Interorient Shipmanagement

    Cyprus's Shipping Deputy Ministry congratulates Interorient Shipmanagement on 40 years in Cyprus. The company manages more than 80 vessels.

  6. 2020

    Interorient Shipping Fund

    The group launches an alternative investment fund authorised by the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission, opening shipping investments to a wider circle of investors.

  7. 2024

    After 45 years, InterMaritime

    Interorient Shipmanagement joins Intership Navigation and Donnelly Tanker Management to form InterMaritime Shipmanagement, managing more than 170 vessels from Interorient's Limassol headquarters.

Shipowning

A fleet in the house colours since 1979

Interorient owns and operates its fleet directly, through joint ventures and through the Interorient Shipping Fund. Today the focus is product tankers and bulk carriers; over the years the fleet has also included containerships, multipurpose and ro-ro vessels. Each ship carries the yellow funnel and the IN monogram, and each is managed within the group to the standards charterers rely on.

Shipowning · Investment fund

Interorient Shipping Fund

A separate company in the Interorient family: an alternative investment fund established in Cyprus in 2020 and authorised by the Cyprus Securities and Exchange Commission. It opens shipping transactions usually reserved for large investment firms, aiming for an annual dividend yield and capital gains from vessel trading and asset appreciation.

  • Ring-fenced investment structures, each holding its vessels through a special purpose vehicle.
  • Second-hand tankers and bulk carriers acquired swiftly, drawing on market expertise and a trusted network across the industry.
  • Vessels managed to maximise yield and preserve asset value.

Ship management · 1979 – 2024

Interorient Shipmanagement

For 45 years, from 1979 until the 2024 merger, Interorient Shipmanagement managed the group's own fleet and ships for third-party owners from its head office in Limassol and its technical and crewing offices in Singapore and Manila, earning its place among the leaders in quality ship management.

Its teams provided technical, crew and commercial management and newbuilding supervision for crude, product and chemical tankers, bulk carriers of all sizes, container ships and general cargo vessels. Interorient also pioneered commercial management pools in specific segments, keeping its managed ships efficiently employed.

InterMaritime Shipmanagement

After 45 years, Interorient Shipmanagement joined Intership Navigation and its sister company Donnelly Tanker Management to form InterMaritime Shipmanagement, headquartered in Interorient's building on Franklin Roosevelt Avenue in Limassol. One manager now looks after more than 170 tankers, LPG carriers, bulk carriers, container ships, multipurpose vessels, cement carriers and car carriers.
